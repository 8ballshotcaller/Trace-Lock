How TraceLock works
  1. Choose a photo — drop one in or tap to pick it.
  2. Position it — pinch to zoom, drag to move, rotate if needed.
  3. Tap "Freeze & Trace" — the screen locks in place.
  4. Trace it — lay paper on the screen and draw. Nothing will move, even if you press hard.
  5. Unlock — hold both bottom corners of the screen together for about a second and a half. A ring will fill clockwise in each corner; once it completes, you're back to editing.
